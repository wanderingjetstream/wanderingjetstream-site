01 Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Wayword app for iPhone, developed by Wandering Jetstream LLC ("we," "us"). It describes what information is collected when you use the app and how it is handled.

What we collect

Wayword does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal information ourselves. Your gameplay data is stored on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Wayword displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect certain information from your device, including:

— Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA), where you have permitted tracking
— IP address and coarse location inferred from it
— Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version)
— Ad interaction data (such as which ads were shown and tapped)

This information is used to serve ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, and detect fraud. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Tracking and your choices

On iOS, Wayword asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking occurs. If you decline, the app will request non-personalized ads, which do not use your advertising identifier for personalization. You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Children

Wayword is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Questions about privacy? Email us at support@wanderingjetstream.com.
