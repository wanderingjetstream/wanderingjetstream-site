Wayword

A spaced-repetition vocabulary app for iPhone. Learn the 5,000 most frequent words in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese, with IPA and audio — and, from version 1.0, build and share short courses on any subject.

No ads and no data collection. Everything stays on your device.

01 What Wayword is

Wayword is a flashcard app built on spaced repetition: it brings each word back just before you are likely to forget it, so your review time goes to the words that need it. Words are ranked by how often they appear in real text, so the first ones you learn are the ones you will meet most.

Six languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese, each with its own core list of the 5,000 most frequent words.

IPA and audio

Every word comes with a translation, its IPA transcription and a simplified pronunciation guide, plus its part of speech, CEFR level and, where the language has one, gender. Hear words spoken aloud, and record your own voice on any card.

Similar Sounds

A listening drill for the pairs that trip every learner up: you hear one word of a near-identical pair and pick which one it was.

Your own decks

Build decks by hand or import them, for an exam, a trip, or a show you are watching. The same spaced repetition runs underneath.

02 Lessons

Coming in 1.0

Tutors and learners can build short courses inside Wayword. Each lesson is 8 to 12 steps, mixing:

  • Pictures
  • Listening
  • Typing
  • Matching
  • Ordering
  • Sorting

A finished course is a single .wayword file that you share by AirDrop or the Files app. Lessons work for any subject, not just languages, and anyone can play a shared course on the free tier.

Read what a .wayword course is, or see Wayword for tutors and teachers.

03 Snapshots

Coming in 1.0

From the Stats screen, turn your progress into a Snapshot: a card you can share with a tutor, a study partner, or your own camera roll. Wayword only shares a Snapshot when you do.

06 Where the words come from

The core word lists are built from real language. Source texts are drawn from Project Gutenberg (via the Gutendex API) and the Leipzig Corpora Collection — both open, well-documented, and licensed for redistribution including commercial use.

Leipzig Corpora Collection data is used under the Creative Commons Attribution licence (CC BY). We gratefully acknowledge Universität Leipzig, the Sächsische Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Leipzig, and InfAI as the source of corpus data used in building these word lists.

No corpus text is included in the app — only frequency-ranked headwords and our own editorial enrichment.

07 Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Wayword app for iPhone and to these Wayword pages. The short version

No ads and no data collection. Wayword makes no network requests of its own, has no accounts, and keeps everything you do in it on your device.

What Wayword collects: nothing

Wayword has no server, no account system, no analytics, and no crash reporting of its own. It does not send your vocabulary, your progress, your courses, or anything else to us or to anyone else. We do not collect your name, your email address, or any other personal information through the app.

Separately from Wayword, iOS lets you choose whether to share crash and usage information with app developers. If you have turned that on, Apple may give us crash reports that do not identify you. You control this in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

What stays on your device

Wayword stores the following on your iPhone, and only there:

  • your decks and the cards in them, including vocabulary you import and any pictures or recordings you add;
  • the courses you build or open, and your progress through them;
  • your review history and spaced-repetition schedule;
  • your progress counts, streaks and statistics;
  • your settings, such as the language you are studying and your appearance and audio preferences;
  • your Wayword Pro purchase status.

None of this is backed up to us, shared with us, or visible to us. It is included in your own iPhone or iCloud backup only if you have turned that on in iOS, which is a matter between you and Apple. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing courses and Snapshots

Wayword only shares something when you do. When you share a course (a .wayword file) or a Snapshot (a progress card from Stats), you choose where it goes with the iOS share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, the Files app, or another app. It travels through that service, under that service's terms, to the people or places you pick. It never passes through us, and we cannot see it.

A shared course contains only what its author put in it, and a Snapshot shows only what is on the card.

No ads, no tracking

Wayword shows no ads. It does not use the Advertising Identifier, never asks for tracking permission, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The app includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK, with its companion User Messaging Platform SDK, as an unused third-party component: Wayword never initialises it, so it sends nothing.

Wayword Pro and purchases

Wayword Pro is a one-time in-app purchase; there are no subscriptions. Apple processes the purchase. Wayword checks it on your device, and we receive no payment details or purchase history about you. Apple's handling of your information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Email you send us

If you email us — for support, or to ask for educator codes — we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as a school address or a link to a tutoring profile. We use it only to answer you and, for educators, to check that you teach and send the codes. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone, and we delete it on request. Email to our support address is routed by Cloudflare Email Routing.

Do not sell or share

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA. We have never done so. Wayword shows no ads, so there is nothing to opt out of, and no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism is required.

Children

Wayword is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email support@wanderingjetstream.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

California residents have equivalent rights to know, delete, and correct, and to opt out of sale or sharing under the CCPA as amended, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Wayword holds no personal data about you on any system we control, so the quickest way to delete your Wayword data is to delete the app, which removes all of it from your device. If you have emailed us, we can delete that correspondence.
