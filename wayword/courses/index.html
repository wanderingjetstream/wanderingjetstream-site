What is a .wayword course?

A .wayword file is a Wayword course packed into one file. You send it like any other document, and whoever receives it opens it in Wayword.

Courses arrive in Wayword 1.0

What is in the file

A course is a short series of lessons. Each lesson has 8 to 12 steps: pictures, listening, typing, matching, ordering, and sorting. The file holds the text, pictures and audio those lessons use — everything needed to play the course — in a single file. A course can be about anything, not just a language.

How to share one

  • AirDrop: share the course from Wayword and choose a nearby iPhone.
  • Files: save it to iCloud Drive, a shared folder, or anywhere else in the Files app, and pass it on from there.
  • Messages, Mail or another messaging app: send it as an attachment, the same way you would send a photo or a PDF.

To open a course someone has sent you, tap the file and open it in Wayword.

Works offline

Once a course is on your phone, it does not need the internet. Wayword makes no network requests of its own, so you can play a course on a plane, on the underground, or anywhere without a signal.

Free, Pro and Creator

Anyone can open any shared course and play the first 3 lessons free; Pro unlocks the rest. Sample courses play in full on the free tier.

Anyone can also build and share a course of up to 3 lessons for free. Creator, a one-time purchase that includes Pro, lets you build and share courses of any length. See Free, Pro and Creator.

Privacy

A shared course contains only what its author put in it. Your progress through a course stays on your phone, and Wayword never sends anything back to the author. If you want your tutor to see how you are doing, send them a Snapshot from the Stats screen.

If you are the author, check a course before you share it: whatever you put in it travels with the file. We never receive courses, so we cannot see, review, or recall one once it has been sent. See the privacy policy and, for who is responsible for a course's content, the terms of use.
