Wayword support

Found a bug, spotted a wrong translation, or have a question? Email us. We read everything and aim to reply within a few days, and always within 30 days for privacy requests.

support@wanderingjetstream.com

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the Wayword version shown at the bottom of Settings, and the language you were studying.

Common questions

How do I restore Wayword Pro?

Open Settings in Wayword and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Pro is a one-time purchase, so restoring it never costs anything.

How do I move to a new phone?

Wayword has no accounts: your decks, progress and courses live on your iPhone, and they move with it when you set up the new phone with Quick Start or from an iCloud or computer backup. Then tap Restore Purchases to bring back Pro.

To move cards by hand instead, use Settings → Export Cards to JSON on the old phone and import the file on the new one. Courses can simply be shared to the new phone again.

How do I share a course?

Share it from Wayword, then choose AirDrop, Messages, Mail, the Files app, or another app. The person receiving it taps the file and opens it in Wayword. More in what a .wayword course is.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Wayword stores is on your device, so that removes all of it; we hold no copy. To remove just a deck or some cards, delete them in the app: they go to Settings → Recently Deleted first, where you can restore them or delete them for good.

If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, ask and we will.

I teach. Is there anything for me?

Yes: Wayword Pro is free for tutors and teachers, with codes for your students. See Wayword for tutors and teachers.

How Wayword handles data is set out in the privacy policy; the rules are in the terms of use.
