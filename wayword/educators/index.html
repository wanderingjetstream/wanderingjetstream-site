Wayword · For educators

Wayword for tutors and teachers

Build a course once and hand it to every student as a file. No accounts, no class codes, and no student data to look after.

Lessons and Snapshots arrive in Wayword 1.0

Build a course once

In Wayword you build short lessons of 8 to 12 steps and group them into a course. Each step is one of six kinds:

  • Pictures
  • Listening
  • Typing
  • Matching
  • Ordering
  • Sorting

Lessons work for any subject, not just languages. When the course is ready, share it as a single .wayword file by AirDrop, the Files app, or a messaging app. Every student who opens it gets the same course, and anyone can play a shared course on Wayword's free tier.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for, on your side or theirs: no class roster, no logins, no passwords to reset, and no student data held anywhere except on each student's own phone. Wayword makes no network requests of its own, so once a course is on a phone it works offline.

Students send you Snapshots

From the Stats screen, a student can make a Snapshot — a card showing their progress — and send it to you the same way you sent the course. You see what they choose to send, when they choose to send it. Wayword never reports anything to you on its own.

Free Pro for educators

If you teach, Wayword Pro is free for you. Email support@wanderingjetstream.com from your school address, or include a link to your tutoring profile (Preply, italki, Wyzant or similar). Tell us roughly how many students you have, and we will send you a Pro code plus codes for your students.

We use your email only to check that you teach and to send the codes. See the privacy policy.

What your students need

  • An iPhone with Wayword installed.
  • The .wayword file you send them.

That is all. For the details of the file itself, read what a .wayword course is.
