Pegwise Support

Wandering Jetstream LLC

Pegwise is a digital cribbage board and peg tracker for keeping score during a game of cribbage. It supports 2–4 players on a single device, with a classic board layout and easy score adjustment.

Need help or have feedback?

Email us at support@wanderingjetstream.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Does Pegwise deal cards or count hands?
No — Pegwise is a scoring board. You play with real cards; Pegwise tracks the pegging.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Pegwise needs no internet connection.
Are my scores saved?
Yes — your current game is saved on your device automatically.
