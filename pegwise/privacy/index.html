Privacy Policy for Pegwise

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Pegwise is a cribbage scoring app published by Wandering Jetstream LLC. We designed Pegwise to work entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. Pegwise does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, and does not connect to the internet. Your game scores and player names are stored only on your device and never leave it.

Third parties

Pegwise does not include third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Children's privacy

Because Pegwise collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data — for example, by adding a feature that connects to the internet — we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy information before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@wanderingjetstream.com.
