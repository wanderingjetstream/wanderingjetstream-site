Pegwise

A clean, modern cribbage board for your phone. Bring it to the table, pass it around, and stop hunting for the pegboard — Pegwise tracks the score so the game stays in your hands.

Download on the App Store — coming soon

What Pegwise does

  • Tracks scores for 2, 3, or 4 players on one device — including in the free version.
  • A smooth, continuous board to 121 — no fiddly pegs, just clear progress at a glance.
  • Tap + to score, press and hold to score fast, − to fix mistakes.
  • Player names and games are saved automatically and restored when you return.
  • Designed for the table: big, legible, and out of your way.

What Pegwise doesn't do

Pegwise doesn't deal cards, count hands, or play for you. You play cribbage with real cards and real people — Pegwise replaces the pegboard, not the game.

Free, fair, and private

The free version is supported by a single banner ad — no popups, no interruptions. A one-time Remove Ads purchase turns advertising off entirely. Pegwise has no accounts and no servers; your games never leave your phone. Read the privacy policy — it's short, because there's not much to disclose.
