Pegwise · iPhone

The cribbage board,
without the board.

Leave the matchsticks and torn envelopes behind. Pegwise is a clean, fast cribbage scoreboard for 2–4 players on a single device — so you can focus on the cards while it tracks the pegging.

01 What it does

  • A classic cribbage board you tap to score — fast and clear.
  • 2 to 4 players on one device.
  • Name your players, or keep the classic colors.
  • Big, easy plus/minus controls so nobody argues about the count.
  • Your game is saved automatically — close the app and pick up later.
  • Works completely offline. No account, no sign-up.

02 What it isn't

Pegwise is a scoreboard, not a card game. You play with a real deck the way cribbage was meant to be played — Pegwise just keeps the score, so the board never gets bumped and the pegs never get lost.

03 Help & privacy

Questions or feedback? Visit Pegwise Support. Pegwise collects no data — read the privacy policy.
