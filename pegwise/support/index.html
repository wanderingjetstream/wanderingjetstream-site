Pegwise Support

A digital cribbage board and peg tracker for 2–4 players on one device.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?
Email support@wanderingjetstream.com — a real person reads every message, and we'd genuinely like to hear from you.

Common questions

Does Pegwise deal cards or count hands?
No — Pegwise replaces the pegboard, not the game. You play with real cards and count your own hands; Pegwise keeps the score beautifully.
How many players does it support?
Two, three, or four players on a single device — including in the free version. Set the player count and names from the settings (gear) button.
How do I score points?
Tap a player's + button to add a point. Press and hold to score quickly. The − button corrects mistakes.
Will I lose my game if I close the app?
No. Your game — scores, names, and player count — is saved on your device automatically and restored when you return.
How do I start a new game?
Tap Reset and confirm. Player names and count are kept; scores return to zero.
How do I remove the ads?
The free version shows a single banner ad. A one-time Remove Ads purchase in the settings turns advertising off entirely. Already purchased on another device? Use Restore Purchases in settings.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your phone. Pegwise has no accounts and no servers — see the privacy policy for the full picture.

Reporting a problem

If something looks wrong, an email with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a quick description (a screenshot helps enormously) will usually get it fixed fast. We're a small studio — your report very likely goes straight to the person who wrote the code.
